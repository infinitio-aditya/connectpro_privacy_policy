# ConnectPro: Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: July 14, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how ConnectPro ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use the ConnectPro mobile application (the "App") and our backend services. 

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of your information as described in this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide our real-time networking and client management services, we collect several types of data:

### A. Personal & Professional Information You Provide
* **Account Information:** Name, email address, password (stored encrypted via secure hashing).
* **Professional Profile Details:** Job title, company name, industry, biography/description, services offered, and professional interests.
* **Social and Contact Links:** Phone number, personal/company website, LinkedIn profile URL, Twitter/X profile URL, and Calendly scheduling URL.
* **Media Assets:** Profile pictures (avatar photos) and promotional brochures (PDF documents) you upload.

### B. Geolocation Data
* **Real-time Location:** With your explicit consent, we access and collect your device’s precise geolocation coordinates (latitude, longitude) and city. 
* **Foreground Location Only:** We do **not** track your location in the background. Your location is updated only while you are actively using the App to pinpoint your profile on the map and calculate distances to nearby professionals.

### C. System-Generated & Activity Data
* **Social Activity:** Connection requests, accepted connections, chat messages, scheduled appointments, and registered event RSVPs.
* **CRM Data:** Contacts grouped under your Client Directory, status flags, and interaction notes.
* **Structured Meetings (Dance Cards):** Meeting goals, accomplishments, networks, and action items logged during your 1-on-1 sessions.
* **Usage Statistics:** Logged statistics including connection counts, weekly message volume, profile views, and earned badges/achievements.

---

## 2. Device & System Permissions We Require

Depending on your mobile platform (iOS or Android), the App will request access to the following permissions:

* **Location Services:** Required to display your profile pin on the map and discover nearby professionals. 
* **Push Notifications:** Required to alert you of new chat messages, incoming connection requests, and meeting reminders.
* **Storage/Photo Library:** Required to let you select and upload a profile picture and brochures from your device storage.

*You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time through your device's operating system settings. Declining location services will disable the interactive map discovery features.*

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:
* **Matchmaking & Local Discovery:** Panning your location on the map to find nearby professionals.
* **Facilitating 1-on-1 Collaboration:** Helping you schedule and track meetings ("Dance Cards") and manage exchange requests ("Asks & Gives").
* **Client Relationship Management (CRM):** Helping you organize your customers, transaction history, and business notes.
* **Communication:** Supporting instant direct messaging and sending push notifications.
* **Security & Verification:** Authenticating your account, resetting passwords, and preventing fraudulent sign-ups.

---

## 4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

We respect your privacy and do **not** sell your personal data. We only share information in the following limited circumstances:

* **Public User Profiles:** Any details you add to your profile (name, title, bio, services, photo, website, and map location) are visible to other logged-in users of the App. Your password and private CRM notes are never visible to others.
* **Service Providers:** We share coordinates and maps data with third-party mapping libraries (e.g., **Google Maps API**) to render the interactive map interfaces. These APIs are bound by their respective privacy terms.
* **Legal Compliance:** We may disclose data if required by law, subpoena, or to protect the safety and security of our users.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

We retain your personal data only as long as your account is active or needed to provide you with App services.

### Account & Data Deletion
Under Google Play Store and Apple App Store rules, you have the absolute right to delete your account and all associated personal data.
* **How to Request Deletion:** You can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by sending an email to our support team at **support@infinitiosoftware.com** with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
* **Retention after Deletion:** Upon receiving a deletion request, we will permanently purge or anonymize your personal profile, credentials, location coordinates, uploaded media, and CRM logs from our active databases within thirty (30) days.

## 6. Security of Your Data

We use industry-standard security measures (including JWT token authentication, HTTPS encryption, and hashed passwords) to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or modification. However, no transmission over the internet or mobile network is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
